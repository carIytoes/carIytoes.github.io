RUN — ARM·01

Robotic Arm Build

Ongoing hobby build
Robotics Arduino Motor control Hardware

Background

Outside of coursework and process-focused research, I wanted a project that kept my hands-on hardware and control-systems skills sharp — the kind of tactile, build-it-yourself work that's easy to lose touch with when most of a semester is spent on theory and simulation. A small robotic arm fit the bill: a well-scoped mechanical and electrical project with plenty of room to go deeper over time.

Approach

The build combines mechanical assembly with microcontroller-driven motor control, building on earlier experience with Arduino and breadboard prototyping. The scope covers both sides of the problem: the physical structure and joints of the arm, and the control logic that drives it — reading inputs, translating them into motor commands, and coordinating multiple joints to move predictably.

Status

This project is still in progress. I'm keeping this page intentionally updated as the build evolves rather than presenting it as a finished product — which honestly says more about how I actually work than a polished final writeup would.
